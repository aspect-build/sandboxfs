A fast FSKit-backed filesystem for staging Bazel sandboxes on macOS.

This installer places

  • sandbox.app in /Applications — hosts the FSKit filesystem module.
  • sandboxfs in /usr/local/bin — the command-line tool.

After installing, a few one-time steps activate the filesystem and wire it into Bazel — the final screen walks you through them.

Beta software (v0.1).
